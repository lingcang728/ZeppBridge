- Added: **Hand a year or your whole history to AI**. Each category can now look back 1 year or All (up to 10 years), and workouts are no longer capped at 90 days; long ranges show how big the task is and whether the AI can read it all.
- Added: **Quarters and years on the card table**. 1 year deals four quarter piles that open into months; All deals one pile per year that opens into quarters. Turn on Range, tap the first and last card, and the whole run goes into the box.
- Added: **Apply to all categories**. Set one category’s range and copy it to the others in one tap, instead of picking days category by category.
- Added: **Backfill right where you need it**. When you pick 1 year or All and this computer’s history is shorter, choose 1 / 2 / 3 / 10 years on the spot and it backfills in the background; the overview asks too when you have less than a year. Months not backfilled yet are drawn dashed, and ones the cloud truly has nothing for say “None in cloud”.
- Added: **Replace a workout with a corrected .fit (#140)**. Pick the file under the workout’s data source; only this computer changes, Zepp cloud stays as it is, and you can restore the cloud version anytime. The file must start within 5 minutes of the original.
- Improved: **Training plans understand trail running and mountain biking (#138)**. They go to the watch as running and cycling; inline skating and cross-country skiing can be written into a plan, but Zepp only accepts running, cycling, pool and open-water swims, and strength exercises and sets can’t be sent.
- Fixed: **Workout pauses lost on some watches (#139)**. Every kind of pause is now kept, exported FIT files carry timer stop events and per-point distance, and per-kilometre splits no longer drift on workouts with many pauses; the first launch after updating re-processes local data in the background.

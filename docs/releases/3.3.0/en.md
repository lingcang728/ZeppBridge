- Added: **Start at login**. Turn it on in Settings › Sync and updates and ZeppBridge opens when you sign in to your computer and syncs as usual; you can also have it stay in the tray without opening a window.
- Added: **Suggestion cards on the home page**. When you have just finished a workout you have not looked back on, slept clearly less than usual, or have not moved for several days, the home page shows a suggestion card; one tap deals the related workouts and recovery into a hand and hands it to your AI. At most two show at a time, a dismissed kind stays away for the rest of the day, and you can turn them off in Settings › AI.
- Added: **Deal cards by question**. Where you hand data to AI, pick a question first (compare the same route, PRs at the same distance, a race review, the nights your recovery was off) and the app picks the matching workouts or nights into a hand; swap any card for one from the reserve and what goes to the AI changes with it.
- Added: **Zoom the workout and sleep lists like a photo album**. Hold Ctrl and scroll, or pinch on a touchpad, to move between the full list, a tile grid and a calendar heatmap; the button at the bottom left switches to cards that sum up each year or month, and a card opens from the one you clicked. Workout rows now show a small route outline, and sleep rows a stage bar on a shared timeline.
- Improved: **Heart rate zones in more places**. Medium and small heart rate cards in workout details are now colored by zone, with a thin zone bar underneath; point at it to see each zone’s share and time. Every row in the workout list carries a zone bar, and the training status page adds weekly zone-time bars that say which zone model they use.
- Improved: **Confirmations stay inside the app**. Deleting, disconnecting or signing out no longer opens a system dialog but a glass panel that matches the interface; irreversible actions are marked in red, and Esc cancels.
- Improved: **One more hint when a sync succeeds but finds nothing**. You may have signed in to a different account than the one your watch is paired with: if that account was created with Google or Apple, add an email to it in the Zepp app first, then sign in with that email.
- Improved: **The light theme is a neutral warm grey**. Backgrounds, text and outlines lose the faint green tint they had.
- Improved: **A redrawn Zepp Cloud icon**. The Zepp Cloud icon in the home page’s data sources is now a vector glass cloud that stays sharp on high-resolution screens.
- Fixed: **Moving from a beta to the matching release now delivers that release’s letter from the author**. Before, only the release notes appeared and the letter was treated as already read.
- Fixed: **Life event names no longer pile up on trend charts**. When several eras sit close together, their names go on two lines, each on its own band; any that still do not fit are left off the chart and still show when you hover.

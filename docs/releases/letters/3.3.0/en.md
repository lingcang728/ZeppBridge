To all my friends using ZeppBridge,

Hello, it's Lingcang.

I hear today is Thanksgiving. Happy Thanksgiving to everyone celebrating today! Whether you celebrate Thanksgiving or not, I'd like to take this opportunity to thank each and every one of you for being part of the ZeppBridge community. Your support, feedback and encouragement mean a lot to me. Thank you for coming along on this journey!

Since the last version I've made quite a few more updates, so we've taken a big step straight into 3.3.0. Inspired by the way personal agents proactively reach out, I added a “Question of the day” to the home page to take some of the friction out of “Send to AI”: based on how your day looks, ZeppBridge picks the one question most worth asking an AI, and you can hand it straight over for analysis. By the way, you can now have ZeppBridge start when your computer starts, too. Every time you boot up, it automatically syncs any new data from the Zepp App, so the moment you wake up in the morning you can see how you slept last night.

I also took up your suggestions about the sleep and workout lists: they used to be so long that finding a particular day was a chore. Now, just like pinching with two fingers in your phone's photo album, you can switch between year, month and day views. On a computer, use Ctrl + mouse wheel (Command + scroll wheel on a Mac) or pinch on your trackpad. On top of that, you can now select several workouts in the list and export them as FIT files in one go, and the import panel has been redone.

The sleep view got a big upgrade as well. Many thanks once again to Felipe Dias for the help: you can now pick last night's wake-up mood and bedtime journal in ZeppBridge and add them as context to the prompt you give the AI, so it can analyse your sleep more accurately. The sleep list has improved a lot too: it's clearer and easier to read, carries much more information, and the whole screen feels more “efficient”. I hope you like the new sleep screens.

I've also smoothed out the expand and collapse animations of a few cards on the overview page. There are fewer dropped frames and less stutter than before (sadly, not none yet), and I'll keep working on it. And some good news: you can now attach a diagnostic report and a structure sample right from the letter page. Click “Sample” and you can send me a single workout directly, which makes it much easier for me to track down problems. Basically, whenever there's something you'd like me to improve, just click “Write to the author” in the top-right corner of the home page. Please keep the suggestions coming, and tell me about any feature you'd like to see; if it's a bug that gets in the way of your daily use, all the more reason to tell me! Your feedback is what keeps me updating ZeppBridge. Thank you!

One more thing: ZeppBridge has teamed up with SparkyFitness. In their next release, they should list ZeppBridge as one of the supported platforms, right alongside Garmin Connect and Apple Health. The author of SparkyFitness is seriously impressive. I learned a lot from working together, and I want to give special thanks for that. It's a great GitHub project with 6.9k stars: it's self-hosted, and honestly, it even has native iOS and Android apps, which is just incredible. If you already know SparkyFitness, this partnership might help round out your own data setup. If anything doesn't line up after the update, just tell me, and I'll work with the SparkyFitness author on how to improve it.

Wishing you all a happy winter!

— Lingcang

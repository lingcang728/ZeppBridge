To everyone using ZeppBridge,

Hello, it's Lingcang.

Quite a few days have passed since my last letter, and in that time I've received far more replies than I ever imagined. Many of you wrote incredibly warm words, and many sent genuinely constructive feedback. It made me very glad I built this feature. For this version, I've answered every reply one by one, so by the time you read this, my answer should already be waiting in your letter box. Do take a look. Whether you send a suggestion or just a thank-you, I'll do my best to write back (in your language). Thank you all for the encouragement!

There are a few important things I want to repeat here, even though I've said them before, because some of you may have missed them.

**Third-party sign-in for Advanced data.** Many of you told me you can't use a third-party account with Advanced data. That's right: it's a limitation of the Advanced data feature itself. With official authorization you can sign in with any third-party account (Google or Apple, for example), but for technical reasons Advanced data only works with a regular Zepp sign-in. I hope you understand.

**Requests for Garmin and Apple Watch support.** Some of you asked me to support Garmin or Apple Watch. Come on: I built ZeppBridge in the first place because Zepp (Huami) has nothing as convenient as Garmin Connect or Apple Health for moving your data around. Asking me to support those is a bit beyond what I had in mind, don't you think?

**The problems with the Linux version.** I know the Linux version still has serious problems. From 3.0.0 to 3.0.6 I tried six different fixes, and every one of them turned out not to work, so this may not be something I can solve on my own. On top of that, I don't have a Linux machine. Without being able to reproduce the problem and test a fix, any fix would be a castle in the air. This may really need a contributor on Linux to send a PR. If one of you finds a way to make the Linux version run smoothly, please open a PR and I'll merge it straight away.

**Questions about sleep duration and activity data.** Some of you said your sleep duration is wrong (time in bed counted as sleep), some said certain activities are calculated incorrectly, and some said only a handful of data types are supported. What I want to say is that these are limits on Zepp's side. I've done everything I can. If data is wrong, incomplete or missing a type, the gap is in Zepp's own data stream, and I can't make up data out of thin air to fill it. I hope you understand.

**Automatic sync in the background.** The new version greatly improves automatic sync in the background. In principle, it should now sync smoothly in the background, without you having to bring the window to the front each time to refresh.

**Support for older Zepp watches.** I'm sorry, but the support I can offer covers every watch that appears in Zepp's official Amazfit store. Those watches have icons and official names, so I can include all of them. For some very old models that are no longer sold, I really can't guarantee full compatibility. If you spot one and let me know, I'll do my best to add it to the list, but it may not be perfect. I hope you understand.

Wishing you all good health and every success!

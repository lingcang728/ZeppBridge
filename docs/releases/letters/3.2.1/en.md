To all my friends using ZeppBridge,

Hello, it's Lingcang. This is a small, urgent bug-fix release.

It started this afternoon, when ten letters suddenly poured into my letter box. I was in the middle of a McDonald's meal, and the Personal Agent on my phone kept buzzing so wildly that I thought there was an earthquake. It turned out to be your feedback, full of enthusiasm. Some of it was sharply worded, of course, and sounded very urgent. So I carried my half-eaten McDonald's back to the computer and started fixing things one by one. Luckily, I managed to get it out before the day was over.

One of you told me the new workout detail page was simply “terrible” (after reading that, I felt pretty terrible too), and asked whether I had put myself in users’ shoes before changing it. Well, I did. You can actually choose between the new layout and the old one in the top-right corner of the detail page. Still, I’ll try to make that clearer in the new version, and maybe make the button for going back a bit bigger. With every big UI change, it’s hard to please everyone. Even Apple was laughed at by hundreds of millions of users when iOS 26 first came out. Sadly, I can’t do better than Apple.

And right after strength training details came out, some of you reported that they didn’t actually work. I dug into it carefully and have tried to push a fix in this version. To be honest, fixing it was about as hard as fixing the stutter on Linux, because I have neither: I almost never go to the gym. What I’m good at is outdoor sports (running, mountaineering, cycling and so on), and with my own data I can tune those really well. As for indoor training, I already said in my last letter that I could never have done it without Felipe Dias (thanks, man, and thank you again for all your work), so for now I can only feel my way around in the dark while I fix it. If anything still falls short, please keep “bombarding” me. Don’t worry, I only eat McDonald’s once a week (okay, sometimes twice).

There is also a big update today that makes replying more useful: you can now add up to 3 screenshots to explain your problem. I think 3 should be enough to describe one problem; if not, you can always write another letter. The reply limit has also gone up to 5,000 characters, and you’ll see how many you’ve written as you type. Once you’ve read this, give it a try. Just a few words is fine: tell me what could be better, or what features you’d like to see.

Looking forward to your replies. Wishing you a pleasant winter.

— Lingcang

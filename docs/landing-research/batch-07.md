## Per-site observations

### Claude

Anthropic’s restrained, warm-paper presentation makes a broad promise—“Think fast, build faster”—then puts a usable entry point directly in the first screen: continue with Google, email or SSO, plus a desktop-app download. The observed heading uses `anthropicSerif`, 72 px, computed weight 330; body/navigation use `anthropicSans` (15 px body and 12 px footer labels). Spacious two-column hero gives way to centered Free / Pro / Max pricing cards, FAQ rows and a dense dark sitemap. The Product menu opens on hover and groups Claude products, capabilities, extensions and models; top-level Developers and Enterprise paths route to technical and organizational detail, while Resources/Pricing handle learning and conversion. Feature and audience detail therefore sits behind an organized product menu; the homepage itself is unusually conversion/pricing-heavy.

No nontrivial motion was detected in the sampled first view; the Product hover does reveal the mega-menu. The captured DOM includes header/nav/main/footer landmarks and a “Skip to main content” link. The page is long but the pricing and FAQ sections have clear labels and separators. Copy reference: “Think fast, build faster.” Sources: [homepage](https://claude.com/), [official interface list](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8114487-what-interfaces-can-i-use-to-access-claude).

## Cross-site principles and transfer to ZeppBridge

1. **Name the user outcome before the feature inventory.** Claude, Goodnotes and Meta AI begin with a plain-language promise. ZeppBridge should lead with a precise result users recognize—bringing their Zepp/Amazfit history into a private local archive—then explain how.
2. **Show the real product early.** Goodnotes pairs its promise with a real notebook/PDF preview; QQ and Edits show product surfaces beside each workflow. Keep ZeppBridge’s interactive sample-data app prominent, label it as sample data, and let it demonstrate a real user path before describing internals.
3. **Group by a few user jobs, then give each job its own explanation.** Goodnotes’ writing/PDF/cross-device tasks and QQ’s five feature families scan quickly. For ZeppBridge, group around connect/import, inspect trends and export/share; avoid creating extra top-level categories for implementation layers.
4. **Make the first CTA specific and honest.** Download, Start Free and Try actions are explicit across the set. Use platform-specific download actions and a sample-data/demo action; state OS support and prerequisites beside each download rather than implying universal compatibility.
5. **Use page depth for proof.** Goodnotes explains distinct workflows with screenshots; Adobe’s official text structure links task-specific features to plan detail. ZeppBridge’s five scroll beats can use source-backed screenshots, concrete data-flow explanation and platform-specific setup pages while keeping homepage navigation short.
6. **Keep privacy and account boundaries visible.** Yandex makes telemetry optional at install, Edits explicitly tells users to log in, and Meta AI shows how its ecosystem connects. ZeppBridge has a stronger local-first story: say where data remains, what optional handoff sends, and that no extra AI/account step is required.
7. **Motion should support orientation.** Yandex’s sheen and Goodnotes’ reveals add polish, but the Edits blob video and SeaArt’s many dynamic assets can fail to render in capture. Favor light, reduced-motion-friendly transitions and keep all essential explanation readable without animation.
8. **Accessibility belongs in the product evidence.** QQ’s sampled page has no H1 and many unlabelled image elements; Meta AI exposes two H1s in its captured markup. Use semantic headings, text alternatives for meaningful product screenshots, keyboard-visible focus and text labels for charts/data states. This batch was a visual/content review, not a full accessibility audit.

**Three strongest design references for ZeppBridge:**

- **Claude** for calm typography, restrained color, and a broad but intelligibly grouped product menu. Transfer the hierarchy and tone; do not inherit its very large sitemap or pricing-first homepage.
- **Goodnotes** for demonstrating a tangible data workspace and explaining workflows in the order users perform them. Transfer its screenshot-plus-short-explanation rhythm and cross-device clarity; retain ZeppBridge’s own warm-paper/olive identity.
- **Edits** for dividing a creator journey into clear stages and giving iOS/Android actions their own labels. Transfer the simple process framing to connect → inspect → export; do not imply ZeppBridge requires Instagram-style account sign-in.

**Patterns to reject:** crowded all-tools homepages and community feeds as the first explanation (SeaArt); unsubstantiated scale, speed or security claims (QQ/Yandex); giant category menus with overlapping labels; autoplay/blob video as the only explanation (Edits); and extra account or AI upsell prompts before a visitor sees how the archive works.

**Concrete transfer:** retain the current five-beat visual rhythm, but make the beats follow one evidence-backed path: connect a supported Zepp source → see real sample records/charts with missing values still visibly missing → understand local storage and optional AI handoff → choose Windows/Android or other supported build → reach the exact install/help instructions. Keep a short header with Product, How it works, Privacy, Downloads and language; use one primary sample-data CTA and platform-specific downloads. Use measured screenshots and short claims linked to their data/source boundaries; avoid invented metrics, testimonials, medical outcomes or partner endorsements.

## Method limitations

Several pages use delayed or embedded media, so only pages explicitly scrolled received post-scroll screenshots; some SeaArt tiles remained blank, and Edits video playback was not verified. Accessibility notes are limited to captured semantic markup and exposed controls; keyboard, screen-reader, contrast and mobile checks were not completed. No performance profiler or controlled cross-run timing test was performed.

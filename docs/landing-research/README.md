# Landing page research — 2026-10-07

User-provided a16z charts rank revenue, mobile activity and web visits, not design quality. Their 150 ranked positions yield 112 distinct product entries (110 brands when OpenAI/ChatGPT and Anthropic/Claude are merged). Research covers every product entry using 10 batches of 10 primary entries, plus 12 supplemental entries.

Collection: 10 GPT-6 Luna agents, high reasoning. Implementation: 2 GPT-6.1 Sol agents, high reasoning. Deployment is pending user review; all changes and previews remain local.

Each batch records dated official URLs, observed navigation and page hierarchy, typography and motion evidence, reusable principles and unsuitable patterns. Unavailable pages and inferred details must be marked explicitly. Rankings are not endorsements of interface quality.
